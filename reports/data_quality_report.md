## Data Quality & Cleaning Report
Generated by `scripts/clean_pipeline.py`. Every transformation below is reversible / auditable against the raw files in `data/raw/`. No row is dropped solely for being "messy" - rows are only removed when a stable identifier cannot be recovered at all, or when they are exact duplicates. Everything else is standardized and, where the raw value could not be trusted, flagged with an explicit boolean column instead of being silently discarded.
